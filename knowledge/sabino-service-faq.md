# Sabino Service and Ordering FAQ

Last reviewed: November 2026. Use these answers for Sabino-specific customer questions. Do not invent unlisted policies.

**Does Sabino have Wi-Fi? What is the Wi-Fi password? Is there internet?** Yes. Sabino has customer Wi-Fi. The current password is **sabinocafe123**. If the network name or password has changed, ask a staff member for help.

**Can I order on the website? How does online ordering work?** Yes. Choose items in the website’s Order section, review quantities and the total, then confirm. The website creates an order number and a QR code. Show the QR code to Sabino staff so they can view and verify the exact order.

**Can staff see my order from the QR code?** Yes. The QR code opens a signed Sabino order summary containing the order number, customer name or reference, chosen items, quantities, total, creation time, and payment status. A changed or fake code will fail verification.

**Can I pay online or in advance?** When Sabino’s verified PayMongo merchant checkout is enabled, the confirmed order can be paid through the secure hosted checkout using the payment methods activated for Sabino. Until the merchant account is connected, the website can create and verify the order QR but cannot accept online payment. Pay at the shop using a method confirmed by staff.

**How do I know my order is correct before paying?** The website always shows a review step with every item, quantity, price, and total. Check the summary and confirm it before the QR code or payment button is created.

**Can I change an order after confirming it?** A confirmed QR represents the items shown at confirmation time. Ask Sabino staff before preparation if you need a change. Create a new order if staff asks you to submit a corrected QR.

**Is an online order automatically accepted or being prepared?** No. The QR records the customer’s confirmed request. Sabino staff must scan and accept it before preparation. Customers should not assume preparation has started until staff confirms.

**Do you offer pickup, delivery, table service, reservations, parking, power outlets, restrooms, accessibility, or a pet-friendly area?** These details are not yet verified in the knowledge base. Call or text Sabino at +63 916 653 9270 before relying on one of these services.

**Can I customize sweetness, ice, milk, syrup, or espresso shots?** Many cafés offer adjustments, but Sabino’s exact modifiers, availability, and extra charges are not confirmed. Ask the barista before paying.

**Do you have allergy, vegan, vegetarian, halal, gluten-free, or nutrition information?** Sabino’s complete ingredient, allergen, dietary, and nutrition list is not yet verified. Tell staff the exact ingredient you must avoid. Shared equipment may cause cross-contact.

**What payment methods does Sabino accept at the shop?** In-store payment methods have not yet been verified. Ask staff whether cash, GCash, Maya, or cards are currently accepted and bring a backup method.

**How long will my order take?** Preparation time depends on queue size, item availability, and order size. Ask staff for the current estimate after they scan and accept the QR.

**Can I order when Sabino is closed?** You may be able to prepare an order summary, but it is not accepted until staff verifies it during operating hours. Reported hours are Monday-Friday 9:00 AM-10:00 PM, Saturday 1:00 PM-9:00 PM, and Sunday closed. Confirm holiday hours.
